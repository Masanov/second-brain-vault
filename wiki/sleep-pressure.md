# Sleep pressure

The homeostatic drive to sleep, which accumulates across waking hours and dissipates during
sleep. Mediated by adenosine building up in the brain as a by-product of cellular energy
use.

This is one of two processes governing sleep timing; the other is the circadian rhythm.
They are independent, which is why one can feel simultaneously exhausted and unable to
sleep.

## Relationship to caffeine

[[caffeine]] blocks adenosine receptors. It does not clear the adenosine, which continues
to accumulate behind the block. When the caffeine is metabolised, the accumulated signal
registers at once — the familiar afternoon crash.

The consequence: caffeine defers sleep pressure rather than reducing it, so the debt is
paid the same day regardless.
